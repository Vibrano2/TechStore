# Product Requirements Document

## Project
Germany Electronics & E-Mobility E-Commerce Platform

## Version
1.0

## Market
Germany

## Primary Language
German

## Secondary Language
English

## Currency
EUR (€)

## Product Vision
Build a trusted German online technology store where customers can discover, compare, evaluate and purchase electronics, smart devices and e-mobility products with clear specifications, secure payments, reliable shipping, warranty information and professional support.

## Core Positioning
Modern technology. Clearly explained. Reliably delivered.

## Target Customers
- Technology shoppers
- E-mobility buyers
- Price-conscious customers
- Early adopters
- Small business buyers

## Initial Business Model
Retailer-first. The company controls suppliers, catalogue, pricing, inventory, fulfilment, warranty and customer support.

Marketplace functionality is explicitly out of scope for the MVP.

## E-Mobility Compliance
Every e-mobility product must clearly show:
- Road legal: Yes/No
- ABE available: Yes/No where relevant
- Insurance required: Yes/No
- Registration required: Yes/No
- Intended use: public road / private property / off-road

Do not market a vehicle as road legal in Germany unless verified.
